# Benchmarks

Before running, grading, or reporting benchmarks, read [docs/BENCHMARKING.md](docs/BENCHMARKING.md). It gives the comparison rules, native-run procedure, blind grading commands, usage format, and reporting commands.

Use the user's task prompt unchanged. Include a parent-only baseline. Keep solving agents away from acceptance checks and other candidates. Let them choose whether to call RepoTracer.

Report group and model settings, full task cost, parent/scout tokens including cache, time, and blind quality grade together. Keep failed and interrupted runs visible. Do not present tuned tasks as independent validation.

Tooling and the guide are public. Store raw runs, label keys, credentials, and private research outside tracked files. Do not modify user copywriting or apply benchmark patches to the working checkout without a request to do so.
