The failure is narrow and repeatable

  1. The previous policy allowed one targeted lookup for a likely single command, function, or file even when the prompt did not name its path.
  2. Routing v2 replaced that boundary with “one known command, function, or file” and foregrounded unknown test locations as a Scout trigger.
  3. json-version names one command but not its source file. Sol inferred discovery work and called Scout first in 3/3 candidate runs.
  4. Those unnecessary calls raised this control's three-repeat cost from $0.1984 to $0.4654 and wall time from 27.0 to 170.0 seconds.

The 84-run lane stops after the first repository operation completes. Cost and time are route-decision measurements, not complete-task effects.
