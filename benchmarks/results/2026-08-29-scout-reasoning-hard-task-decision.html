RepoTracer benchmark decision · 29 August 2026

Keep Luna medium.

High reasoning produced one extra official pass across six held-out complete tasks. It did not meet the preregistered rescue gate, ran slower on both repositories, and used 3.9× the Scout requests.

Decision
Retain medium globally. Do not add adaptive high.

High had a real result on recursive delegation, but the signal did not repeat on the harder cancellation task. Blind review traced most failed patches to Sol's implementation rather than weak Scout localization. Raising Scout reasoning is the wrong fix.

What blind review found

  • All 12 patches were scored without arm identity. Official verifier results were also recorded.
  • Seven cases used Scout evidence strongly. Five others had no usable Scout handoff or were attributed directly to parent execution.
  • The strongest failed medium patches had accurate cross-component maps but missed implementation invariants such as emitting the recursive tool result or compiling the cancellation machinery.
  • One Boa high pass succeeded after Scout timed out. That is evidence for Sol's fallback execution, not high Scout reasoning.

Scope and limits

Two repositories and three repeats per arm are enough to apply this decision gate, not enough to estimate a general pass rate. One reviewer scored each blinded case. The study compares medium and high only; the prior 72-run Scout-only matrix already rejected low for cross-file completeness defects.
