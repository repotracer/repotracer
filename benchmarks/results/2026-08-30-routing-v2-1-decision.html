The corrected boundary

  1. Classify the request, not the search plan. A single command, function, symbol, file, or localized behavior starts with one targeted lookup. Unknown paths do not make it broad.
  2. Delegate explicit breadth. Exhaustive relationships, inventories, or behavior that propagates across owners call Scout first.
  3. Anchor both sides with examples. “Add JSON output to the version command” stays local. “Locate every caller of exported timeout settings” calls Scout.

The distinction is ownership breadth. The rejected v2 text let Sol invent a broad search plan for a narrow request, so json-version called Scout in 3/3 runs. V2.1 kept it local in the 3-run pilot, the 3-run recheck, and four new pathless-local holdout categories.
