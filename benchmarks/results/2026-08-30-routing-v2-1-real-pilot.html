Decision

Proceed to the next work item on routing correctness. Do not claim a stable cost or latency improvement from this three-pair pilot. All six runs passed both rounds and both inactivity verifiers.

Across three pairs, v2.1 median cost was $2.0973 versus $3.7470 for v1 (−44.03%), while median wall time was 580.8s versus 476.6s (+21.87%). Totals were $10.9730 versus $9.6856 (+13.29%) and 2,428.0s versus 1,487.8s (+63.19%), driven by repeat 2.

What caused the outlier

The repeat-2 v2.1 Scout call cost only $0.0029. The parent Sol conversation cost $6.8375 across 98 requests and took 1,164.4s for implementation. The increase was not Scout replacing less Sol work; it was parent-side request volume and latency. Repeat 1 showed the intended direction: one Scout call, lower total cost, and modestly higher wall time.

Quality and evidence limits

  • v1 and v2.1 each completed 6/6 rounds and passed 6/6 verifier commands.
  • Each v2.1 run made one Scout call with 5/5 valid citations.
  • This is one real task across three paired repeats, not a repository-wide effect estimate.
  • Next work may proceed for routing correctness; investigate parent-side outliers before making economics claims.
