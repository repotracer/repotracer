Scout tools / final decision

Remove CodeGraph.

It consistently reduced prompt usage, and the revised policy selected it correctly. It still failed the quality floor at low and high reasoning. RepoTracer keeps native Read, Glob, and Grep only.

decision

Delete the adapter and its public configuration seam. Do not auto-index, retain an opt-in, or replace the failed quality result with a larger-model requirement. The native control remains the product path.

03The selection fix worked; the tool still lost

01
Original policy

CodeGraph was unnecessarily selected in 5 of 6 exact negative controls. Native tools kept higher final-answer quality.

02
Revised policy

One explicit rule reserved graph use for cross-file structure, request flow, and change impact. It chose CodeGraph on 18 of 18 suitable trials and skipped 6 of 6 exact controls.

03
Reasoning sweep

Three repeats per task at low, medium, and high reasoning tested whether more model effort recovered quality. Low and high still trailed control.

04
Clean cutover

The adapter, dynamic app-server tool bridge, repository-tool schema, and explorer.codegraph setting were removed. No compatibility shim remains.

04Evidence and limits

  • Primary tool matrix. 72 blinded trials: eight tasks, three repeats, control/placebo/CodeGraph. Quality, path recall, citation validity, usage, tool activity, and wall time were retained.
  • Follow-up matrix. 144 control/CodeGraph trials across low, medium, and high reasoning. All processes succeeded; every citation set validated.
  • Scope. This is one repository and eight retrieval tasks. It supports the adapter decision, not a general claim that graph retrieval never helps.
  • No hidden dollar claim. The report leaves subscription usage in native counters. Complete provider cost remains separate production-Gigo evidence.
