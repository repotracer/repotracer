The identity for a read-only repository scout. Cool graphite and bone, one teal accent used as a highlighter, IBM Plex throughout, and three motifs pulled straight out of the mark. Full rules in BRAND.md; tokens in brand/tokens.css.

A 5×5 field of files, most of it dim. A trail of dots ascends through it, each larger than the last, terminating in a single solid teal hit. The repository stays untouched, one path gets followed, one location comes back verified — and the growing dots give the mark a direction.

Fieldr 2.4 · 20% opacity · 20 dots
Trail4 dots on the diagonal · radii 3.0 → 5.0 · always ascending

Clear space on every side equals the lattice pitch — 15% of the mark's width. The wordmark is IBM Plex Sans SemiBold at −0.022em, outlined to paths in every shipped file, so it never depends on a font loading.

Accent Deep#0A6E66Accent as text on bone
Muted#5F6670Secondary text, light
Verify#0A6E66Passed, cheaper — deep accent

DoKeep one accent per viewport. If two things are accent, neither reads as the answer.
DoUse Accent Deep when the accent has to carry words on a light ground.
Don'tAdd a second accent. Teal is the only chromatic color in the system; warn red is semantic.
Don'tReintroduce the warm paper ground. These neutrals are cool on purpose.
