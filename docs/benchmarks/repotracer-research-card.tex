\sectiontitle{Not supported yet}
\colorbox{amberwash}{\parbox{\dimexpr\linewidth-2\fboxsep\relax}{
\textbf{No universal savings percentage.} The paired economics repeat one repository question.\par
\textbf{No claim that total tokens always fall.} In the optimized median pair, all-model input rose 58.80\% while cost fell.\par
\textbf{No latency win.} Median wall time rose 31.21\% in the optimized cohort and 6.65\% under immediate routing.\par
\textbf{No local-model win.} FastContext 4B Q4 produced only one usable handoff in three bounded runs and remains disabled by default.}}
